📚 Learn & Documentation

Getting Started Guide

1️⃣ Create Your First Customer

Go to the Product tab and use the Create Customer form to add a new customer to your database.

Fill in: Name, Email, Company

2️⃣ Search for Customers

Use the search feature on the Home page to find customers by name, email, or company.

Search by: Name, Email, or Company

3️⃣ Update Customer Information

Click on the Update Customer form in the Product tab to modify existing customer details.

Provide: Customer ID and new details

4️⃣ Delete a Customer

Use the Delete Customer form to remove a customer from the system (irreversible action).

Required: Customer ID only

❓ FAQ

What is AI CRM Assistant?

AI CRM Assistant is an intelligent customer relationship management system powered by artificial intelligence to help you manage and track customer information efficiently.

How do I create a new customer?

Go to the Product tab, fill in the Create Customer form with name, email, and company, then click Create.

Can I recover deleted customers?

No, deletion is permanent. Make sure you have the correct customer ID before deleting.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all customer data is stored securely and protected with industry-standard security measures.
